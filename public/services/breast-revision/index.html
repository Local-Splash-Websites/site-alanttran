Breast Procedures

Breast Revision

Breast revision surgery is performed to improve or correct the results of a previous breast procedure. Patients may seek breast revision for cosmetic concerns, implant-related issues, or changes that occur over time due to aging, pregnancy, or weight fluctuations.

Dr. Tran customizes breast revision procedures to address each patient’s unique anatomy and goals.

You may be a candidate for breast revision if you experience:

  • Implant rupture or leakage
  • Capsular contracture (scar tissue tightening around the implant)
  • Implant malposition or asymmetry
  • Rippling or contour irregularities
  • Changes in breast appearance over time
  • Dissatisfaction with previous breast surgery results
  • Desire to change implant size or type

Breast revision consultation

At your consultation we will discuss:

  • Your concerns and goals
  • Previous breast surgeries and implant history
  • Medical history, medications, and allergies
  • Breast anatomy and implant condition
  • Surgical options and expected outcomes

Dr. Tran will carefully evaluate your concerns and develop a customized revision plan.

Breast revision procedure

Depending on the patient’s needs, breast revision surgery may involve:

  • Implant replacement or removal
  • Correction of scar tissue
  • Repositioning implants
  • Breast lift techniques
  • Correction of asymmetry or contour irregularities

Preparing for your breast revision surgery

Before surgery, patients may be instructed to:

  • Obtain imaging studies or mammograms if necessary
  • Stop smoking before and after surgery
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications and supplements
  • Arrange transportation and postoperative assistance

What to expect after your breast revision surgery

Following surgery, patients receive detailed postoperative instructions regarding:

  • Surgical bra or compression garment usage
  • Incision care and activity restrictions
  • Medications and follow-up appointments
  • Recovery expectations and healing timeline

Recovery varies depending on the extent of revision surgery performed. Swelling, bruising, and temporary discomfort are common during the healing process.
