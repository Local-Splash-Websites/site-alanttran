Face Procedures

Neck Lift

A neck lift is a surgical procedure designed to improve visible signs of aging in the neck and jawline. By removing excess skin and tightening underlying muscles, a neck lift can create a smoother, firmer, and more defined neck contour.

You may be a candidate for a neck lift if:

  • You have loose or sagging skin beneath the chin
  • You notice excess fat or “banding” in the neck
  • You have loss of jawline definition
  • You want a smoother, younger-looking neck contour
  • You experience visible signs of aging in the lower face and neck

Neck lift consultation

At your consultation we will discuss:

  • Your cosmetic goals and expectations
  • Medical history, medications, and allergies
  • Previous surgeries or treatments
  • Skin elasticity and neck anatomy
  • Surgical options and expected outcomes

Dr. Tran will also review before and after photos to help you better understand your potential results.

Preparing for your neck lift procedure

Before surgery, patients may be instructed to:

  • Obtain medical evaluations or lab testing
  • Stop smoking before and after surgery
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications and supplements
  • Arrange transportation home after surgery
  • Prepare for recovery at home

Neck lift techniques

Traditional neck lift

Incisions are placed around the ears and sometimes beneath the chin to tighten neck muscles, remove excess fat, and eliminate loose skin.

Liposuction

For patients with excess fat and good skin elasticity, neck liposuction may help create improved neck and jawline definition.

Platysmaplasty

This technique tightens the neck muscles to reduce visible banding and improve contour.

Incision closure

After reshaping the neck and tightening tissues, the incisions are carefully closed with sutures and dressings are applied.

What to expect after your neck lift procedure

Following surgery, patients receive detailed postoperative instructions, including:

  • Neck care and incision management
  • Prescriptions for medications
  • Follow-up appointments
  • Activity restrictions during recovery

Neck lift recovery

Swelling and bruising are expected during the early healing phase. Most patients return to work and light activities within one to two weeks, though strenuous activity should be avoided for several weeks.

Neck lift results

As healing progresses, patients notice a smoother, firmer neck contour and improved jawline definition. Neck lift results are long lasting and help restore a more youthful appearance.
