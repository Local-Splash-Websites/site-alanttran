Face Procedures

Face Lift

A facelift, also known as rhytidectomy, is a surgical procedure designed to restore a more youthful appearance to the face by addressing sagging skin, deep folds, and loss of facial definition caused by aging.

A facelift can improve the lower face and jawline while creating smoother, firmer contours for a refreshed and natural-looking appearance.

You may be a candidate for a facelift if:

  • You have sagging skin in the cheeks or jawline
  • You notice deep folds around the nose and mouth
  • You have jowls or loss of facial definition
  • You want a more youthful facial appearance
  • You have loose skin and reduced elasticity due to aging
  • You desire long-lasting facial rejuvenation

Face lift consultation

At your consultation we will discuss:

  • Your aesthetic concerns and goals
  • Medical history, medications, and allergies
  • Previous cosmetic procedures or surgeries
  • Skin quality and facial anatomy
  • Surgical options and expected outcomes

Dr. Tran will review before and after photos to help you understand what facelift surgery may achieve.

Preparing for your facelift procedure

Before surgery, patients will receive detailed preparation instructions, including:

  • Obtain any required medical evaluations
  • Stop smoking before and after surgery
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications and supplements
  • Arrange transportation and postoperative assistance
  • Prepare for recovery time at home

Face lift techniques

Traditional facelift

Incisions are typically placed around the ears and hairline to tighten deeper facial tissues, remove excess skin, and restore youthful contours.

Mini facelift

A mini facelift uses shorter incisions and is often recommended for patients with early signs of aging and mild skin laxity.

SMAS facelift

This advanced technique addresses the deeper support structures of the face for longer-lasting and more natural-looking results.

Incision closure

After repositioning the tissues and removing excess skin, the incisions are carefully closed with sutures. Dressings may be applied to support healing.

What to expect after your facelift procedure

Following surgery, patients receive detailed recovery instructions, including:

  • Incision care and postoperative guidelines
  • Prescriptions for medications
  • Follow-up appointments
  • Activity restrictions during healing

Face lift recovery

Swelling, bruising, and mild discomfort are common during the initial recovery period. Most patients return to light daily activities within two weeks, though complete healing continues over several weeks.

Face lift results

As swelling resolves, patients notice smoother contours, improved jawline definition, and a more youthful appearance. Facelift results are long lasting, though the natural aging process continues over time.
