Earlobe Repair

Earlobe repair is a minor surgical procedure designed to restore the appearance and shape of damaged, stretched, or torn earlobes. Earlobes may become elongated or split due to heavy earrings, trauma, aging, or gauging.

Dr. Tran performs earlobe repair procedures with careful attention to symmetry and natural appearance, helping patients restore confidence and comfortably wear earrings again.

You may be a candidate for earlobe repair if:

  • You have a torn or split earlobe
  • Your earlobe has become stretched from heavy earrings
  • You have enlarged piercing holes
  • You wish to reverse earlobe gauging
  • You have asymmetry or deformity of the earlobes
  • You want a more natural and youthful earlobe appearance

Earlobe repair consultation

At your consultation we will discuss:

  • Your concerns and desired outcome
  • Medical history, medications, and allergies
  • Previous ear procedures or injuries
  • Treatment options and healing expectations

Dr. Tran will evaluate the condition of your earlobes and recommend the most appropriate repair technique.

Preparing for your earlobe repair procedure

Prior to the procedure, patients may be instructed to:

  • Avoid smoking before and after treatment
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications if medically appropriate
  • Clean the treatment area thoroughly
  • Arrange transportation if sedation is planned

Earlobe repair techniques

Torn earlobe repair

The damaged edges of the earlobe are carefully removed and reconstructed with sutures to restore a natural contour.

Stretched piercing repair

Enlarged piercing holes are surgically corrected to improve the appearance and structure of the earlobe.

Gauge repair

For stretched gauge openings, excess tissue may be removed and reshaped to restore the normal shape of the earlobe.

Incision closure

Once the repair is complete, the incisions are carefully closed with fine sutures to promote optimal healing and minimize visible scarring.

What to expect after your earlobe repair procedure

After the procedure, you will receive postoperative instructions that may include:

  • Wound care instructions
  • Prescriptions for medications if needed
  • Follow-up appointments
  • Activity restrictions during healing

Earlobe repair recovery

Most patients experience minimal discomfort and return to normal activities within a few days. Mild swelling and redness are common during the initial healing period.

Earlobe repair results

As healing progresses, the earlobes regain a more natural appearance and improved symmetry. In many cases, ears may be safely re-pierced several weeks after healing is complete.
